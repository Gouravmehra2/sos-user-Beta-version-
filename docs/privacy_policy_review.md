# Privacy policy publication review

The bundled policy is a draft based on customer-app code. It is not a verified statement of backend operations or a determination of legal compliance.

Before publication, the operator must verify and complete:

- Legal entity name, business address, privacy contact email, and any required grievance contact. No verified contact was present in the repository. The email support form currently navigates to a success screen without sending a request; it must not be presented as a working privacy request channel.
- Effective/revision date after approval; no arbitrary effective date has been added.
- Actual server logs, analytics, advertising, data sales/sharing, SDK collection, recipients, and purposes. The draft only names integrations visible in the app and does not promise that data is never sold or used for advertising.
- Retention periods by data category, deletion procedures, backups, storage countries, international transfers, and contractual safeguards.
- Applicable jurisdictions, lawful bases where required, privacy rights, response deadlines, identity verification, and an operational access/deletion request channel.
- Children's eligibility and handling of children's information.
- Actual security controls; the draft makes no unsupported encryption or certification claims.
- Notification/marketing practices and how material policy updates are communicated.

Keep `assets/html/privacy_policy.html` as the single source for policy text displayed in the app.

Drafting reference: FTC guidance emphasizes accurate disclosure of actual information practices and honoring privacy promises: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/consumer-privacy
